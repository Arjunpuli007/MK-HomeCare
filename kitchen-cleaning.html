Designed for kitchens with utensils on the counter, items in cabinets, and appliances in use. Everything in the Occupied Kitchen Clean, plus a dedicated chimney deep clean. Chimneys accumulate thick grease inside the motor chamber, fan blades, and filters — reducing suction and creating a fire hazard. We remove filters for a full soak, degrease inner walls and blades, and restore full suction efficiency.

💡 Ideal for: Kitchens where the chimney hasn't been cleaned in 3+ months
  • Everything in Occupied Clean
  • Chimney interior degreasing
  • Filter removal & soak clean
  • Chimney exterior polish
  • Mesh/baffle filter restoration
  • Suction efficiency check
