Our cleaning process

  1. 1

    Pre-Treatment of Stains

    Stubborn stains and hard water deposits are pre-treated with targeted cleaning agents before scrubbing.

  2. 2

    Top-Down Scrubbing

    We clean from ceiling fixtures and walls down to floors, preventing re-contamination of cleaned areas.

  3. 3

    Fixture & Fitting Polish

    Taps, showerheads, towel rails and all chrome fittings are polished to a streak-free shine.

  4. 4

    Disinfect & Deodorise

    Final disinfectant application and deodorising spray leave your bathroom hygienically clean and fresh.
